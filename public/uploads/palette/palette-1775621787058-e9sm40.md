# Design System Document: Precision Suspension Experience

## 1\. Overview \& Creative North Star

**Creative North Star: "The Kinetic Authority"**

This design system moves beyond the standard "automotive shop" aesthetic to create a high-end, editorial-grade digital experience for Ávila Posto de Molas. We reject the generic grid in favor of **Kinetic Layouts**—where the UI mimics the tension, strength, and curved precision of heavy-duty suspension systems.

The experience is defined by:

* **Intentional Asymmetry:** Using diagonal "shear" lines (reminiscent of the branding) to break the viewport and guide the eye toward action.
* **Technical Depth:** A dark, immersive environment that utilizes metallic textures and tonal layering rather than flat outlines.
* **Robust Authority:** Heavyweight typography that communicates the reliability of industrial-grade engineering.

\---

## 2\. Colors: The Metallic Dark-Mode

Our palette is rooted in a "Deep Chrome" philosophy. We use dark, high-contrast surfaces to make our brand greens and blues vibrate with energy.

### Core Tokens

* **Primary (Action/Brand):** `#a7c8ff` (Light Blue) transitioning to `#002d5c` (Primary Container). Use these for depth and focus.
* **Tertiary (The "WhatsApp" Green):** `#66df6e`. This is our high-priority signal color. Reserved strictly for conversion and success states.
* **Background:** `#121416` (Deep Obsidian).

### The "No-Line" Rule

**Prohibition:** 1px solid borders for sectioning are strictly forbidden.
**The Standard:** Boundaries must be defined solely through background color shifts. A `surface-container-low` section sitting on a `surface` background provides all the separation needed. This creates a "machined" look where parts fit together seamlessly.

### Signature Textures \& Glass

* **The Chrome Gradient:** For hero sections and primary CTAs, use a linear gradient from `primary` to `primary\_container` at a 135-degree angle.
* **The Glassmorphism Rule:** Floating navigation or overlay cards must use `surface\_container` with a 12px `backdrop-blur` and 60% opacity. This prevents the UI from feeling "heavy" despite the dark palette.

\---

## 3\. Typography: Industrial Elegance

We use a pairing of **Work Sans** (for Authority) and **Inter** (for Technical Precision).

* **Display-LG (Work Sans, 3.5rem):** Used for "Hero" headlines. Must always be Bold or Black weight. This is the "voice" of the machine.
* **Headline-MD (Work Sans, 1.75rem):** Section titles. Often paired with a subtle metallic text-shadow or a diagonal background element.
* **Body-LG (Inter, 1rem):** Primary reading. Inter provides a clean, neutral balance to the aggressive display type.
* **Label-MD (Inter, 0.75rem):** Used for technical specs, part numbers, or "Quality Guaranteed" badges.

\---

## 4\. Elevation \& Depth: Tonal Layering

We do not use shadows to create "lift"; we use light to simulate "material."

* **The Layering Principle:** Stacking follows a logical hierarchy of light.

  1. **Level 0 (Base):** `surface` (`#121416`)
  2. **Level 1 (Sections):** `surface\_container\_low`
  3. **Level 2 (Cards):** `surface\_container`
  4. **Level 3 (Pop-overs/Modals):** `surface\_container\_highest`
* **Ambient Shadows:** If a card must float (e.g., an "Urgent Contact" FAB), use a shadow tinted with `#003061` at 8% opacity. Never use pure black shadows.
* **The "Ghost Border":** For input fields or secondary buttons, use the `outline\_variant` token at 20% opacity. It should be felt, not seen.

\---

## 5\. Components: Engineered Precision

### Buttons (Kinetic CTAs)

* **Primary (The Lead):** Deep blue gradient (`primary` to `primary\_container`). Large padding (16px 32px), `md` (0.375rem) roundedness.
* **Action (WhatsApp):** `tertiary` background with `on\_tertiary` text. Incorporate a subtle "pulse" animation on the shadow to denote urgency.
* **The "Suspension" Hover:** Upon hover, buttons should not just change color—they should shift 4px vertically with a "spring" (elastic) easing curve.

### Cards (The "Modular Block")

* **Style:** No borders. Use `surface\_container\_low`.
* **The Diagonal Accents:** Cards should feature a 2px diagonal metallic strip (using `secondary`) in the top-right corner to echo the brand's diagonal motif.

### Inputs \& Fields

* **State:** Standard inputs are `surface\_container\_highest` with a 1px "Ghost Border."
* **Focus State:** The border glows with a 2px `primary` bloom.

### Custom Component: "The Spring Progressor"

Used for multi-step service bookings. Instead of a line, use a series of curved arcs (molas) that "compress" as the user moves through the steps.

\---

## 6\. Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

* **Use Intentional Asymmetry:** Align text to the left but allow background "spring" curves to bleed off the right edge of the screen.
* **Embrace High Contrast:** Keep text `on\_surface` (White/Silver) against the Deep Blue backgrounds for maximum legibility.
* **Apply Metallic Accents:** Use subtle CSS linear gradients (Silver to Grey) on icons to give them a 3D, machined feel.

### Don't:

* **Don't use "Flat" Blue:** Always use the surface tiers. A flat `#003061` background feels like a template; a layered `surface-dim` to `surface-container` feels like a brand.
* **Don't use Rounded-Full:** Avoid pill-shaped buttons unless it's a small chip. Use `md` (0.375rem) for a more industrial, stable feel.
* **No Divider Lines:** If you feel the need to add a line between items in a list, increase the `body-sm` spacing by 8px or shift the background tone instead.
